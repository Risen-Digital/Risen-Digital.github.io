Your Endowment Page Still Names the Director Who Retired

A small, scoped fix for Dakota Zoo: two pages on dakotazoo.org still send donors and event visitors to a director who left the role six months ago.

What We Found

A quick, honest look at dakotazoo.org as it stands today

HIGH

The Endowment Page Names the Retired Director

dakotazoo.org/donate/endowment/ reads, word for word, "please contact Terry Lincoln, Zoo Director at 701-223-7543." Jackie Peeler became Zoo Director in May 2026, succeeding Terry after his 40-year run (KFYR-TV, Sept. 22 and Oct. 22, 2026). That's roughly six months during which the page asking people to give toward the endowment has pointed them to someone who no longer holds the job.

MEDIUM

Same Stale Name on the Zoo Boo Event Page

dakotazoo.org/special_events/zoo-boo/ also quotes Terry Lincoln by name and title, for an event dated October 24, 2026. The contact page itself lists only a general phone number — there's no other page that would catch the stale reference.

A Fix Scoped to the Actual Problem

Not a redesign — a targeted correction on the two pages that ask for money and attention

Two Pages, Corrected

Swap Terry Lincoln's name and contact routing for Jackie Peeler's on the endowment page and the Zoo Boo event page — the two spots we found this live today.

A Site-Wide Sweep

A quick check across the rest of dakotazoo.org for any other page still naming the former director, so every "contact the director" path reaches the current one.
