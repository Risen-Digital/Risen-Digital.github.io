Our Mission

Arts as the engine of a connected Zebulon

“The Zebulon Downtown Arts Council (ZDAC) leverages the visual and performing arts to create a connected, vibrant and inclusive community, catalyze economic revitalization, and promote the talents, diversity and creativity of the Zebulon community.”

The idea for an arts center in Zebulon began in 2011, when the United Arts Council of Raleigh and Wake County proposed arts-development plans for eastern Wake County towns. A steering committee of private citizens formed in response — leading to ZDAC's official establishment as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and, since then, public art projects, community events, and quarterly exhibitions across downtown Zebulon.
