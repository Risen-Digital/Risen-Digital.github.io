Your Front Door Shouldn't Depend on Who Holds the Chair

A small, focused fix for Augusta Downtown Alliance: keep your events calendar and directory current no matter who is — or isn't — in the Executive Director seat.

What We Found

A quick, honest look at augustadowntown.com as it stands today

HIGH

The Events Calendar Shows Nothing

augustadowntown.com/events-calendar/ currently lists zero upcoming events, for an organization whose entire mission is downtown community engagement. That page is often the first thing a member or visitor checks.

HIGH

Three Directors in Thirteen Months, None Right Now

Public reporting shows the Executive Director seat has turned over three times since mid-2025 and is currently empty. Whoever maintained the calendar and directory each time has changed with it — that is a fragile way to run a public-facing site.

MEDIUM

No Named Staff Contact

The Contact page lists only a general form, phone number and email — reasonable during a leadership transition, but it means there is nobody specific keeping the rest of the site current either.

A Fix Scoped to the Actual Problem

Not a redesign — a small piece of automation that keeps working through leadership changes

Calendar That Updates Itself

Connect the Events Calendar page to one shared input the board already controls (a shared Google Calendar or the Alliance's own social posts), so it shows current events automatically — no one has to remember to update the website by hand.

A Directory That Survives Staff Turnover

The same approach keeps a member/business directory page current without depending on any one staff member being in place to update it.

Built to Outlast Any Single Hire

The whole point is that this keeps running whether the Executive Director seat is filled, vacant, or filled by someone new next year.
