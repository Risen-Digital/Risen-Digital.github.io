Our Story

65 years on the Missouri River

The Dakota Zoo opened to the public on June 3, 1961, with 75 mammals and 23 birds on 15 developed acres — grown from the Marc & Betty Christianson farm, which began as an informal animal refuge. Today the zoo sits on 90 acres, is accredited by the Association of Zoos and Aquariums (since 1991), and is supported by roughly 3,400 member households.

Open seasonally; current winter-season hours are Friday–Sunday, 1:00–5:00 PM. Programs include camps, field trips, animal encounters, venue rentals, and signature events like Brew at the Zoo and Zoo Boo.
