Your Members Page Doesn't Require a Login

A small, scoped fix for Hillsboro Symphony Orchestra: members.hillsborosymphony.org loads in full for anyone with the URL, no sign-in required.

What We Found

A quick, honest look at members.hillsborosymphony.org as it stands today

HIGH

No Login Gate on the Members Subdomain

A direct, unauthenticated fetch of members.hillsborosymphony.org loads the page in full — no username, no password, no session. We re-checked this ourselves today (2026-11-01) with a status-code check only; we have not opened, saved, or retained any of the material on the page.

MEDIUM

Several Categories of Material Sit Behind That Same Open Door

We are deliberately not listing specifics here — see the findings note below for exact categories, with nothing reproduced. The short version: this is board- and member-facing material that should require a login, and currently doesn't.

A Fix Scoped to the Actual Problem

Not a redesign — an access-control cleanup on one subdomain

One of Two Straightforward Approaches

Either a proper login gate on the subdomain itself, or moving the sensitive items behind one. We'd scope the exact approach with the board once we know which fits how the page is used today.

Fast, Because the Scope Is Small

This is an access-control fix on one subdomain, not a rebuild or a security audit of the rest of the site.
